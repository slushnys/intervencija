Before we begin

A few useful
answers.

Can you take a project from architecture through delivery?

Yes. Critical Builds covers design and implementation through to production readiness and handover. We agree the scope, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria with you at the start.

Can you work with our existing engineering team?

Yes. The engagement can sit alongside your engineers and delivery partners, using agreed review points and ownership. Fractional Architecture is designed for ongoing support; a focused build can cover a specific system or integration.

What if the scope is still taking shape?

Start with the business goal and the questions you need to resolve. We can shape a focused review or discovery scope before committing to a larger build.

How are projects priced?

Fees reflect the scope, complexity, dependencies, and delivery responsibilities. The proposal sets out the commercial model, deliverables, and assumptions before work begins.

What should we share in the first message?

Your intended outcome, a short description of the systems involved, and any important timing. There is no need to share source code, customer data, or detailed internal documents at this stage.
