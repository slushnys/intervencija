How we work

Decisions you can
explain and defend.

Every significant recommendation is written down: the context, the options considered, the decision and its consequences. Leadership can see why, engineers can see how, and whoever inherits the system can see what was traded off.

  1. 01

    Understand the whole picture

    Establish the business goal, current systems, dependencies and constraints with the people closest to the work.

  2. 02

    Make the important decisions explicit

    Compare options, document trade-offs, and agree scope and success criteria before committing to implementation.

  3. 03

    Deliver in useful increments

    Validate assumptions early. Plan testing, integration, deployment and operational readiness alongside the software.

  4. 04

    Build continuity into the handover

    Document decisions and the operating model, share knowledge, and agree ownership for what comes next.

Before we begin

Questions worth
asking early.

Procurement, security or commercial questions not covered here? Email hello@intervencija.eu

Can you take a project from architecture through delivery?

Yes. Critical Builds covers design and implementation through to production readiness and handover. Scope, responsibilities and acceptance criteria are agreed at the start.

Can you work alongside our engineering team and partners?

Yes. Engagements are designed to sit alongside your engineers and delivery partners, with agreed review points and clear ownership. Fractional Architecture provides ongoing support; a focused build can cover a specific system or integration.

What if the scope is still taking shape?

Start with the business goal and the questions you need answered. A focused review or discovery phase can shape the scope before you commit to a larger build.

How are engagements priced?

Every engagement is priced in a written proposal before work begins. Reviews are typically a fixed fee for a fixed scope, builds are planned around milestones with acceptance criteria, and ongoing architecture support runs at an agreed cadence. The proposal sets out deliverables, assumptions and how changes are agreed.

Can we put an NDA in place first?

Yes. An NDA can be agreed before you share anything sensitive. A first conversation needs no source code, customer data or internal documents.

Who owns the work you produce?

You do. Code, documentation and decision records produced for you are delivered into your repositories and tools, and the agreement assigns ownership of them to you.

How do you handle access to our systems?

We agree the minimum access each scope needs, work within your security policies and tooling, and hand access back when the engagement ends.

Where are you based, and how do you work across time zones?

Intervencija is based in Vilnius, Lithuania, and works with organisations internationally. Meeting rhythm and overlapping working hours are agreed as part of the proposal.

What should we share in the first message?

Your intended outcome, a short description of the systems involved, and any important timing. There is no need to share source code, customer data or detailed internal documents at this stage.
