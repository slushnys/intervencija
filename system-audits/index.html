What you receive

Inside the report

Evidence first.
Then recommendations.

A typical audit report follows this outline. The agreed scope sets the depth, and every finding links back to the evidence behind it.

  1. Executive summary

    The decision, the recommendation and the risks that matter most, on a page leadership can act on.

  2. Current architecture

    Systems, interfaces, data flows and ownership, mapped at the level the decision needs.

  3. Findings and risk register

    Each finding with its evidence, likelihood, business impact and a suggested owner.

  4. Options and trade-offs

    Realistic alternatives compared on cost, risk, time to value and reversibility.

  5. Decision records

    The recommended direction, with its assumptions stated so they can be tested.

  6. Sequenced roadmap

    The next phase broken into steps, with validation points and dependencies.

Before we begin

Useful details.
Clear expectations.

Do we need complete documentation?

No. Existing diagrams and documents are a useful starting point, but a review can also draw on interviews, repositories, interfaces and operational evidence. Access and expectations are agreed before work starts.

How much access will you need?

It depends on the question. We agree the minimum information and access the review needs, work within your security processes, and an NDA can be agreed before anything sensitive is shared.

Is this a security or compliance certification?

No. This service reviews software and architecture within an agreed technical scope. Penetration testing, formal certification, legal review and regulatory opinions require their own specialist scope.

Why is it free?

It is how we start working with new clients. You see the quality of our findings before you pay for anything. If you then want us to do the work, we propose an engagement with a fixed monthly fee.

What does the free audit cover?

One system and the question you need answered about it. Several systems, or a review for an investor or acquirer, get a fixed-fee quote before work starts.

Does an audit commit us to anything?

No. The report is yours to keep and use, with us or with another supplier.
