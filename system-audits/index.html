Before we begin

Useful details.
Clear expectations.

Do we need complete documentation?

No. Existing diagrams and documents are a useful starting point, but a review can also draw on interviews, repositories, interfaces, and operational evidence. Access and expectations are agreed before work starts.

How much access will you need?

It depends on the question. We agree the minimum information and access needed for the review, taking account of your confidentiality and security processes.

Is this a security or compliance certification?

This service reviews software and architecture within an agreed technical scope. Penetration testing, formal certification, legal review, and regulatory opinions require their own specialist scope.

Does an audit commit us to implementation?

No. The findings are intended to support your decision and your team’s next steps. Any implementation engagement is agreed separately.

How are duration and fees agreed?

The proposal reflects the number of systems, depth of investigation, dependencies, and availability of the people involved. Scope, timing, and commercial terms are agreed before the review begins.
