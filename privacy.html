Privacy notice.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is operated by Intervencija, MB, a small partnership registered in the Republic of Lithuania under company code 305284493 and based in Vilnius. Intervencija, MB is the controller of the information described here. For any question about this notice or your information, email hello@intervencija.eu.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form collects your name, work email address, the service you are interested in, and the message you write. Your company name and preferred timing are optional. You can also contact Intervencija directly by email instead of using the form.

How the information is used

Information you send is used to review your enquiry, assess whether a conversation would be useful, respond to you, and maintain the resulting correspondence. It is processed because you asked us to respond, as a step before any agreement, and in our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries. It is not used for advertising, sold, or added to mailing lists.

Form delivery

Enquiries submitted through the form are delivered by Formspark, a form-processing service, which stores the submission and passes it to Intervencija. Submissions that appear automated may be held by its spam protection. If you prefer not to use the form, email hello@intervencija.eu.

Call booking

If you book a call, the booking is made on Cal.com, a scheduling service, which processes the details you enter, such as your name, email address and answers, and passes them to Intervencija. Cal.com’s own privacy policy applies to its pages.

Hosting and website services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, it receives technical request information such as your IP address, browser details and the time of the request. Fonts and other assets are served from this website.

To count visits, Intervencija uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, provided by Cloudflare, Inc. It receives technical information such as the page address, the referring page, the browser type and the country of the request. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and does not follow you across other websites. Intervencija does not use advertising trackers or behavioural profiling on this website, and sets no cookies.

How long information is kept

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as it is needed to respond and to maintain any resulting business relationship. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time, unless it must be retained for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information you have shared, or object to its use, by emailing hello@intervencija.eu. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Lithuania, this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate.
