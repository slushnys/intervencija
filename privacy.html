Privacy notice.

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form can collect your name, email address, role, company, and the message you provide. You may also contact Intervencija directly by email instead of using the form.

How the information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to review your enquiry, assess whether a conversation would be useful, respond to you, and maintain the resulting correspondence.

Form delivery

The website currently uses a third-party form-delivery service to transmit enquiries to Intervencija. Information entered into the form is sent to that provider for delivery. If you prefer not to use the form, email hello@intervencija.eu.

Website services

The site is hosted through external infrastructure and loads typography assets from Google Fonts. Those services may receive ordinary technical request information, such as an IP address, browser details, and request time. Intervencija does not currently use advertising trackers or behavioural profiling on this website.

Questions and requests

To ask about information you have submitted, request a correction, or ask that correspondence be removed where appropriate, contact hello@intervencija.eu.
