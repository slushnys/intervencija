Before we begin

Useful details.
Clear expectations.

Can you build alongside our engineering team?

Yes. Responsibilities, code review, delivery tools, and integration points can be agreed around your existing team and partners.

What if we only have a business idea?

We can begin by shaping the requirements, identifying the critical assumptions, and defining a focused first scope before estimating the full build.

Can you work with legacy systems?

Yes. We start with the interfaces, dependencies, and operational constraints that shape the change. The approach may be an integration, an extension, or a phased replacement.

How do you manage changes to scope?

Changes are discussed in terms of their effect on the outcome, dependencies, effort, and delivery plan. The commercial proposal sets out how changes are agreed.

What happens after delivery?

Documentation, knowledge transfer, and an agreed handover are part of planning the engagement. Any continuing support, maintenance, or further development is scoped explicitly.
