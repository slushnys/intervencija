Critical Builds

Critical software, carried deliberately into production.

Focused architecture and implementation for workflows, integrations, internal platforms, and data capabilities that carry operational or commercial importance.

Production from the outset

The build is shaped by the operation it must enter.

Fit
Clear boundaries, integration contracts, data ownership, and responsibilities.
Control
Deployment, monitoring, rollback, failure handling, and operational feedback.
Continuity
Documented decisions, operating guidance, and a handover that respects the existing team.

Appropriate initiatives

Defined capability with a real path into operation.

Operational workflows

Approval, fulfilment, reporting, service, or revenue workflows that need a dependable system boundary.

Integration layers

Reliable movement between finance, CRM, ERP, payments, carriers, products, and partner systems.

Internal platforms

Focused tools that give operators clear ownership, better decisions, and fewer manual transitions.

Data capabilities

Pipelines, reporting layers, search, automation, or governed AI foundations tied to a defined use.

Delivery model

A focused production path, integrated with the organisation.

The engagement combines architectural direction and hands-on implementation. Work is sequenced around operational risk and the points where early evidence matters.

  1. 01 - Frame

    Define the capability.

    Clarify the workflow, system boundary, operating responsibility, and production outcome.

  2. 02 - Prove

    Test the sensitive path.

    Use real interfaces and representative data to reduce the most consequential uncertainty early.

  3. 03 - Build

    Deliver the production slice.

    Implement with deployment, monitoring, failure handling, and operational feedback included.

  4. 04 - Transfer

    Establish clear ownership.

    Share the decisions, operating model, maintenance context, and the next sensible sequence.

Production standards

Reliability is designed into the delivery, not added at the end.

Observable

The system can explain its state.

Useful logs, metrics, traces, and business signals are shaped around the operation and its likely failure modes.

Recoverable

Change has a controlled path.

Deployment, rollback, data movement, and fallback decisions reflect the consequence of interruption.

Owned

Responsibility remains clear.

Documentation and operating guidance support the people who will continue to evolve the capability.

Collaboration

Designed to work alongside the team already carrying the business.

The build does not create a parallel technical world. Architecture decisions, interfaces, delivery practices, and handover are shaped with the people who will operate and extend the system.

Boundaries

A Critical Build is defined, not unlimited.

The engagement works best when the capability and production outcome are clear enough to govern. Large portfolio transformation, round-the-clock managed operations, formal certification, and unrelated staff augmentation require separate scope or specialist partners.

Questions

Before delivery begins.

Can Intervencija join an existing engineering team?

Yes. The model is intended to integrate with existing technical and operational ownership. Responsibilities and decision boundaries are made explicit at the start.

Can the build begin with uncertainty?

Yes, when the uncertainty can be framed and reduced through evidence. A System Audit may be the better first step when the decision itself is still unclear.

What happens after handover?

Ownership can remain with the internal team, continue through a defined follow-on scope, or be supported through Fractional Architecture. The intended model is agreed before production handover.
