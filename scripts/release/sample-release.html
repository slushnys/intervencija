Card payments: failure reasons recorded for every attempt

Engagement: Product deliveryObjective: Reduce failed card payments, milestone 2 of 5Released: 21 October 2026

What changed

Every failed card payment now records the reason the bank gave, for example insufficient funds or an expired card. The retry rules in milestone 3 need this data to decide when a retry can work. Customers see no change.
