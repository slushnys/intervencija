Capability statementOctober 2026

Software architecture and engineering for systems your business can’t afford to get wrong.

Intervencija is a software architecture and engineering consultancy for enterprises and scale-ups. We help CTOs and engineering leaders make high-stakes technical decisions and deliver the systems behind them. Every engagement starts with a written proposal: scope, deliverables, fee and acceptance criteria agreed before work begins.

Services

System Audits

An independent senior review before a replatform, migration, acquisition, funding round or build-versus-buy decision. Also used for technical due diligence.

  • Architecture and dependency map
  • Risk register backed by evidence
  • Options, trade-offs and decision records
  • A sequenced plan for the next phase

Format: fixed scope and fee, agreed upfront.

Critical Builds

Architecture and hands-on delivery for platforms and integrations that move money, orders or customer data.

  • Production software with automated tests
  • Integrations with explicit failure handling
  • Deployment, monitoring and runbooks
  • Documented decisions and a structured handover

Format: milestones with agreed acceptance criteria.

Fractional Architecture

Senior architecture leadership, part-time and embedded in your team’s rhythm, for organisations outgrowing their architecture.

  • Design reviews at agreed points
  • Decision records and engineering standards
  • A maintained technical roadmap
  • A sounding board for engineering leaders

Format: ongoing, at an agreed cadence.

How we engage

Contracting
Master services agreement with a statement of work per engagement, on our paper or yours. An NDA can be signed before anything sensitive is shared.
Governance
Named engagement lead, written status at an agreed cadence, live risk and decision log, regular sponsor reviews.
Security
Least-privilege access per scope, work inside your tooling and policies, access returned at the end. Security questionnaires are part of onboarding.
Data protection
EU company working under the GDPR. A data processing agreement can be put in place when personal data is in scope.
Ownership
Code, documentation and decision records delivered into your repositories, with ownership assigned to you.
Commercials
Fixed fees for reviews, milestone pricing for builds, agreed cadence and fee for ongoing architecture.
